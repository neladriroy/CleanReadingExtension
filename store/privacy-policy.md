# Privacy Policy for Clean Reading Extension

Last Updated: 11/02/2025                    

## Introduction
Clean Reading ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Chrome extension.

## Data Collection
Clean Reading operates with complete privacy in mind:
- We DO NOT collect any personal information
- We DO NOT track your browsing history
- We DO NOT store any user data on external servers

### Local Storage
The extension stores only:
- Your preferences (ad blocking, reader mode settings)
- Whitelisted websites
- Custom element hiding rules
All data is stored locally in your browser and never leaves your device.

## Data Usage
The stored preferences are used solely to:
- Apply your chosen settings across websites
- Remember whitelisted sites
- Maintain your custom hiding rules

## Data Security
Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- No third parties have access to your data
- Your settings remain private to your browser

## User Rights
You have the right to:
- Clear all stored data through browser settings
- Disable or remove the extension at any time
- Access and modify all settings through the extension interface

## Changes to This Policy
We will update this Privacy Policy as needed. Significant changes will be notified through the extension's update notes.
