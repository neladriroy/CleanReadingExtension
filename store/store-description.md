# Detailed Description
Clean Reading is a powerful browser extension that enhances your online reading experience by removing distractions and giving you complete control over webpage content.

Key Features:
• Ad Blocking: Automatically removes advertisements and promotional content
• Element Hiding: Select and hide any distracting elements with a simple click
• Reader Mode: Transform articles into a clean, readable format
• Site Whitelisting: Easily disable features on trusted websites
• Custom Controls: Fine-tune your reading experience

Perfect for:
✓ Reading articles and blog posts
✓ Research and studying
✓ Distraction-free browsing
✓ Professional work environment

How It Works:
1. Enable ad blocking to remove unwanted advertisements
2. Use the element selector to hide specific distracting content
3. Toggle reader mode for a clean, focused reading experience
4. Whitelist your favorite sites with a single click

The extension is lightweight, privacy-focused, and doesn't collect any personal data.
